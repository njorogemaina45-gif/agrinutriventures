About the Principal Consultant

Dr. Anderson N. Maina, PhD

Animal Bioscientist · Lecturer · Managing Director, AgriNutri Ventures Ltd

Dr. Anderson N. Maina is a distinguished animal nutritionist and bioscientist with a decade-long career spanning rigorous doctoral research, internationally recognised postdoctoral training, academic teaching, and applied consultancy across Kenya, Canada, and South Africa. He is the Managing Director and Chief Scientist of AgriNutri Ventures Ltd — a livestock and agribusiness consultancy headquartered in Kenya — and holds part-time academic appointments at Murang'a University of Technology (Department of Plant and Animal Sciences) and JKUAT.

He earned his PhD in Animal Biosciences from the University of Guelph, Canada, under the mentorship of Prof. Elijah G. Kiarie, where his doctoral research generated foundational comparative data on specialty protein ingredients in broiler starter feeding programmes, informing both commercial and scientific practice in the global poultry industry. His doctoral work examined the impact of partial soybean meal replacement with select specialty protein ingredients on lifetime growth performance, organ weights, intestinal morphology, plasma biochemistry, and litter attributes in broiler chickens.

Following his doctorate, Dr. Maina completed postdoctoral research at North-West University, South Africa (Food Security and Safety Niche Area, Faculty of Natural and Agricultural Sciences), further broadening his expertise in eubiotics, nutri-epigenetics, and broiler breeder nutritional programming. This emerging field examines how the nutritional status of parent birds shapes the epigenetic landscape, immunity, and productive performance of their offspring — a frontier area with enormous implications for Kenyan poultry production systems.

Beyond broilers, Dr. Maina has conducted landmark research on Black Soldier Fly Larvae (BSFL) meal as a sustainable fishmeal replacer in African catfish (Clarias gariepinus) and Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) diets. His 168-day feeding trials demonstrated that BSFL meal inclusion not only maintains growth performance but improves the proximate composition and essential amino acid profile of fish — a breakthrough for sub-Saharan African aquaculture where conventional fishmeal is expensive and frequently adulterated. He has also contributed original work on Nile crocodile (Crocodylus niloticus) offal meal as a novel protein substrate in Jumbo quail diets — pioneering the use of waste by-products as value-added feed ingredients in Kenya.

His widely cited review on eubiotics in non-chicken poultry (NCP) species — covering quail, turkey, ostrich, waterfowl, and guinea fowl — provides the first consolidated scientific framework for responsible antibiotic reduction across Kenya's diversifying poultry sector. He has additionally published on Omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids (α-linolenic, EPA, and DHA) in livestock and poultry, DHA enrichment of eggs using algal oils, and hydrolysed whole yeast as a prebiotic for broiler breeder pullets — placing AgriNutri Ventures at the intersection of functional nutrition, sustainability, and product quality enhancement.
